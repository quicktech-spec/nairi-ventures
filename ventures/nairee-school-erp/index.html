THE CORE CHALLENGE

Running a school should not mean running after paperwork

Many schools still depend on registers, spreadsheets and phone calls. Attendance sits in one place, fee records in another, and marks in a third. Parents are left guessing, teachers repeat the same entries, and principals lack a clear picture of how classes and teachers are performing.

Nairee School ERP replaces this scattered setup with one system where every role sees exactly what it needs.

ROLE-BASED ARCHITECTURE

Four portals, one connected system

Everyone signs in from the same login page. The system recognises the role and opens the right portal automatically.

Admin / Principal

Create and manage all accounts, set up classes, timetable and fee structure, post announcements, track fee collection, and view the performance of teachers and students across the school.

Teacher

Mark attendance, share notes and study material, assign homework, schedule tests and enter marks, update syllabus progress, and review class performance.

Student

See notes and materials, attendance, homework, test results, timetable, syllabus progress, school and bus timings, and the school calendar. Each student sees only their own data.

Parent

Follow your child's progress, attendance, test results, fee structure and dues, syllabus completion, timetable and holidays. Parents see only their own child's records, and can switch between children from one account.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

What is a school ERP?

A school ERP is software that manages a school's daily operations, such as admissions, attendance, fees, timetable, exams and communication, in one system instead of separate registers and spreadsheets.

Who gets a login, and how are IDs created?

The school admin creates accounts for teachers, students and parents. When a student account is created, a linked parent account is created for that student.

Can a parent see other children's information?

No. A parent can only see their own child or children.

Does it work on mobile phones?

Yes. The portals are designed to be fully usable on phones as well as computers.

Can parents track fees and syllabus progress?

Yes. Parents can see fee structure, amount paid and dues, and how much of each subject's syllabus has been completed.
