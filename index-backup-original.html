The questions you're actually asking

"AI generated video always looks fake. Why would this be different?"

AI gets us to a fast first draft; a person edits and finishes everything before you see it. You're getting speed on production, not a robot's raw output as the final product.

"How is this so much cheaper than an agency?"

Agencies price around headcount and hours. AI cuts the hours on script drafts, initial edits, and variations, so the savings come from efficiency, not from cutting what you actually see.

"What if I've been burned by a 'marketing agency' before?"

You'll see the actual plan before any money changes hands, so you're deciding on something real, not a pitch.

"Do I need all three, or can I get just one?"

You can start with one. We'll tell you honestly which matters most for where your business is right now.
